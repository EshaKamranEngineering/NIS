Technology Overview

The Non-Intrusive Sensor (NIS) detects electromagnetic activity generated by electrical current flowing through the turbine output cable.

Because the system operates externally, the sensor can gather useful monitoring information without directly interrupting the conductor.

The waterproof housing protects the electronics while the wire connection transfers the signal to the monitoring system.
